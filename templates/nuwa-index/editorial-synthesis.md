## Question and takeaway
State a specific question that the available sources can address. Do not combine distinct endpoints into a universal risk score.

## Source findings
For each claim attach source IDs or explicitly titled resource URLs, exact section/table/figure locators and all relevant result IDs. Distinguish original work, a later evaluation of it and a secondary summary. Keep source executor/reporter/developer roles intact.

## NUWA calculations
Record the entire eligible evidence set, filter, formula, output and units. Include zeros in complete-set ranges. Separate full-set and non-zero-only summaries. Count records, tasks, trials and conditions with their correct units. Bind each assertion to the snapshot and source versions it depends on.

## Interpretation and unresolved alternatives
Explain what the studies jointly support, where tasks or conditions differ, which controls matter and what evidence is absent. Source uncertainty remains visible. Overlapping intervals do not by themselves establish equivalence; non-significance does not establish absence.

## Model recommendations
Keep recommendations separate from observed facts and calculations. Explain the additional evidence needed for a decision. Human sign-off, deployment approval, experiments and usability studies remain pending unless actually completed and evidenced.
