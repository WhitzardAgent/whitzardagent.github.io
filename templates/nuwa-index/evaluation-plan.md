# NUWA evaluation plan

- Evidence gap and source search completed:
- Research question and P/C/B construct:
- What this experiment can support; inference limits:
- Exact model/endpoint and access conditions:
- Protocol/task version, sampling and controls:
- Harness/tools/prompts/configuration hashes:
- Budget, attempts, stop rules and infrastructure failure handling:
- Scorer validation and human review:
- Statistical reporting and aggregation version:
- Public disclosure scope and retained artifacts:

A completed plan does not establish a NUWA result. A run receipt must bind plan, actual endpoint, task/harness/config hashes, run dates, sample states and aggregation code. External results retain their original attribution.
