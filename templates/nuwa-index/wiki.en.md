Keep new substantive prose in the private research directory until human editorial approval. This template does not establish source reading, evidence approval or publication approval. Record these stages separately. Choose sourceScope from full-text, abstract, metadata or unavailable; state which sections were actually read.

## Takeaway
Give the reader one specific conclusion supported by the reviewed material. Separate the original authors’ conclusion from NUWA interpretation.

## Research question and design
Explain the question, evaluated system or argument, task, supplied information, comparison or negative control, and success criterion. Keep unknown fields explicit. Do not reconstruct missing methods from another paper with a similar title.

## Representative case
Use a real public source example or a clearly identified source-described workflow. State the starting information, objective, observable endpoint and what the source actually shows. Do not invent individual test cases from aggregate evaluation rows. Summarize protected or hazardous material at a non-operational level.

## Findings and interpretation
Explain what the source finds and how the design supports it. Include consequential null findings and controls. Label NUWA calculations with their complete evidence set and formula. Numerical results remain in the shared catalogue; avoid an independently maintained score copy.

## Limits and connections
State source-specific validity limits, configuration dependence, missing observations and possible alternative explanations. Cite related works for the relationship actually supported; do not imply a matched comparison when only scope differs.

## Decision relevance
Label any proposed action as a model recommendation. State which decision the evidence informs and which inferential step remains unsupported. Do not present an experiment plan as an executed experiment or a safety argument as a certification.
