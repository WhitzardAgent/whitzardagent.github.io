# Independent source review

Bind the review to sourceVersionId, canonical JSON packageHash and contractVersion. Verify exact data plus substantive meaning, omissions, roles, dates and limitations. Accepted individual observations do not certify the whole document or reproduce an experiment.

Record approvedObservationIds, approvedClaimIds, protocol/variant/model mappings with evidence, origins and actual executors, imputation status and unresolved dependencies. Provide idiomatic bilingual claim text. Public issue summaries must exclude hidden/quarantined values and private paths.

Corrections preserve history: explicitly withdraw/supersede results or revise/withdraw unsupported mappings. New package/source content invalidates the receipt. An incomplete or failed extraction never becomes a completed scientific review merely because it parsed.
