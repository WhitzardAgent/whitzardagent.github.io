## Overview brief

Write two or three findings using `title`, `body`, and `resultIds`. Each result ID must belong to this analysis's bound evidence set. Lead with a finding supported by the measured data. Keep a relevant experimental limit beside it; avoid generic caveats that could be pasted onto any study. Specify the next observation that could change the interpretation.

The chart may display a filtered subset while the analysis remains bound to the complete stated scope. Do not generate new conclusions from arbitrary user filters. Unapproved notes remain in the private local-review store.
