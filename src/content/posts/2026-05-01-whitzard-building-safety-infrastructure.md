## Why Safety Infrastructure

Frontier AI systems are no longer just chatbots. They are autonomous agents that can execute code, browse the web, call APIs, and chain complex multi-step actions. This shift from passive models to active agents changes the safety landscape fundamentally.

You cannot evaluate an agent the same way you evaluate a chatbot. You cannot secure a runtime the same way you secure a static model. And you cannot monitor autonomous behavior with tools designed for human-in-the-loop systems.

## What We Are Building

Whitzard builds safety infrastructure across four areas:

### Evaluation

We develop evaluation methods and benchmarks for frontier AI capabilities and risk thresholds. This includes the Nuwa Agent Safety Framework, which provides a structured approach to evaluating AI agent safety.

### Runtime Defense

We build runtime safety infrastructure for AI agent tool calls. AgentGuard, our flagship open-source project, provides zero-trust access control, policy enforcement, tracing, and audit for AI agent actions.

### Risk Monitoring

We track frontier AI risk signals and collect governance evidence. Our risk monitoring work helps identify emerging threats before they become systemic failures.

### Governance

We support evidence-based AI governance through research, evaluation data, and policy-relevant analysis.

## The Nuwa Connection

Nuwa Frontier AI Safety Lab is our research lab. Nuwa develops the research agenda and evaluation methodology. Whitzard builds the safety infrastructure that turns these ideas into usable systems.

Nuwa studies frontier AI risks; Whitzard builds the tools to manage them.

## Open Source

We believe safety infrastructure should be open and auditable. AgentGuard and our evaluation tools are open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/WhitzardAgent).
