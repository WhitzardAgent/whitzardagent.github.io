We are excited to introduce **Nuwa Frontier AI Safety Lab**, a research lab dedicated to studying frontier AI risks, agent safety, and controllable AI.

## Why Nuwa

As frontier AI systems become more capable and autonomous, the risks of loss of control, deceptive alignment, and autonomous misuse grow correspondingly. Understanding and mitigating these risks requires dedicated, rigorous research.

Nuwa is inspired by the myth of Nüwa repairing the sky — we study how to identify and repair safety gaps in advanced AI systems before they become systemic failures.

## What We Study

Our research focuses on five directions:

- **Agent Behavior Safety**: Understanding risks in autonomous AI agent behavior, including deception, goal misalignment, and tool misuse.
- **Frontier AI Risk Evaluation**: Developing evaluation methods and benchmarks for frontier AI capabilities and loss-of-control risks.
- **AI Control and Loss-of-Control Risk**: Investigating control methods and conditions under which advanced AI systems may evade oversight.
- **Runtime Safety Infrastructure**: Building runtime safety infrastructure for AI agent tool calls.
- **Risk Monitoring and Governance Evidence**: Collecting and analyzing risk signals for evidence-based AI governance.

## Supported by Whitzard

Nuwa is the research lab supported by Whitzard, an AI safety startup building evaluation, runtime safety, and risk-monitoring infrastructure for advanced AI systems. Nuwa develops the research agenda and evaluation methodology; Whitzard builds the safety infrastructure that turns these ideas into usable systems.

We look forward to sharing our research and collaborating with the global AI safety community.
